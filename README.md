# Adrak for Windows and Mac

**[Download Adrak](https://snwaf929-cpu.github.io/adrak-releases/)**, or straight away:
[Windows](https://github.com/snwaf929-cpu/adrak-releases/releases/latest/download/AdrakApp-win-Setup.exe) ·
[Mac](https://github.com/snwaf929-cpu/adrak-releases/releases/latest/download/AdrakApp-osx-Setup.pkg)

Adrak is a learning app for Windows 10 and 11 and for Macs with Apple silicon: interactive lessons with maps, code,
diagrams, math and quizzes, a library of ready-made lessons, and an AI Agent that writes new lessons, practice and exams
for what you are learning.

## Download

Open **[the download page](https://snwaf929-cpu.github.io/adrak-releases/)** and press **Windows** or **Mac**. It
shows what to do after the download, in English or Arabic. Or download the installer straight away:

- **Windows 10 and 11:**
  [AdrakApp-win-Setup.exe](https://github.com/snwaf929-cpu/adrak-releases/releases/latest/download/AdrakApp-win-Setup.exe).
  It installs Adrak for your Windows account, with no administrator rights needed. The first time, Windows may say
  "Windows protected your PC", because the installer is not code-signed yet. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer):**
  [AdrakApp-osx-Setup.pkg](https://github.com/snwaf929-cpu/adrak-releases/releases/latest/download/AdrakApp-osx-Setup.pkg).
  The first time, macOS may say it can't check the installer, because Adrak isn't signed by Apple yet. Click **Done**,
  open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, and click **Open Anyway**.

Adrak keeps itself up to date: it looks here for new versions and offers to restart into them. The Mac version has the
Agent, lessons and Settings; practice and exams come in a later update.

## Your learning

What you learn stays on your computer: in `%LocalAppData%\Adrak` on Windows, and in
`~/Library/Application Support/Adrak` on a Mac. In Settings you can link a GitHub repository of your own to keep it in
sync between your computers. Uninstalling Adrak leaves it in place.

This repository holds only the installers, their updates and the download page.
