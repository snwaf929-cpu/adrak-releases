Adrak runs on computers: open this page on your Windows PC or Mac to download it.

After it downloads

  1. Open AdrakApp-win-Setup.exe from your Downloads.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. Adrak isn’t code-signed yet.
  3. Adrak opens by itself. Go to Settings → AI and connect an AI model with your own key.

After it downloads

  1. Open AdrakApp-osx-Setup.pkg from your Downloads.
  2. If macOS says it can’t check it, click Done, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, and click Open Anyway. Adrak isn’t signed by Apple yet.
  3. Open Adrak from Applications, then go to Settings → AI and connect an AI model with your own key.
